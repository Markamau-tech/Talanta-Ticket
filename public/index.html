Kenya’s New National Stadium

The Raila Odinga International Stadium at Talanta Sports City is a world-class 60,000-seat venue built for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Featuring hybrid pitch, VAR, goal-line technology and 52 sky boxes, it will host its first-ever match with this historic friendly.

Located along Ngong Road in Nairobi, the stadium stands as a tribute to the late former Prime Minister Raila Odinga and a symbol of Kenya’s sporting ambition.
